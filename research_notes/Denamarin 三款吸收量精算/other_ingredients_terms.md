# Denamarin A / B / C for a 9.1 kg dog: the ingredients besides SAMe and silybin (mainly phosphatidylcholine), how much the dog absorbs, and milk-thistle / SAMe terminology in English and Chinese

Checked 2026-11-09/10. This builds on round 1 (`../Denamarin 三款成分与吸收对比/product_specs.md`). Round-1 facts are re-cited only where they are needed here.

**Products and the dog's daily dose** (label values; official pages re-downloaded this round):
- **A, Denamarin coated tablet, Medium Dogs.** 1 tablet/day = 225 mg SAMe + 82 mg SPC (24 mg silybin A+B).
- **B, Denamarin Chewable, all sizes.** 1 tablet/day (16–30 lb band) = the same 225 / 82 / 24 mg.
- **C, Denamarin Advanced Chewable, Small-to-Medium.** ½ tablet/day (15–25 lb band) = 60 mg SAMe + 59 mg SPC (17.5 mg silybin). A whole tablet is 120 / 118 / 35 mg.
- **The dog:** 9.1 kg, about 620 kcal ME/day (figure supplied by the coordinator). 9.1^0.75 = 5.24, so intake ≈ 118 kcal/kg^0.75.

**Evidence-grade tags**
- **[A-dog]** Measured directly in dogs; peer-reviewed.
- **[B-human] / [B-rat]** Measured directly in humans or rats (radiolabel or clinical); peer-reviewed; extrapolated to dogs.
- **[C-label]** Official product label, supplier specification, pharmacopoeia, or regulatory drug label.

## Q1. What is the silybin–phosphatidylcholine complex (SPC), and how much PC is in each tablet and each daily dose?

### Takeaway
**No label states PC separately.** All three labels give only the total SPC mass and the silybin it provides.

**The SPC looks like Indena's Siliphos.**
- Denamarin's silybin share of SPC is 29.3% in A and B (24/82) and 29.7% in C (35/118).
- Indena's Siliphos® specification is "≥29.7% to ≤36.3% of silybin by HPLC", so the Denamarin ratios sit at its minimum.
- Siliphos is described as a "1:2 ratio of silybin:phosphatidylcholine", made with soy phospholipids that are "mainly phosphatidylcholine".

**Best estimate of PC (as phospholipid, mostly PC):**

| Product | Per tablet | Range | Per day for this dog |
|---|---|---|---|
| A or B | about 55 mg | 52–58 mg | about 55 mg |
| C | about 79 mg | 75–83 mg | about 39 mg at ½ tablet (range 38–41.5 mg) |

Nutramax does not disclose whether the PC comes from soy or sunflower.

### Inferences
**1. Nutramax states silybin at Indena's guaranteed minimum.** [C-calc]
- Label silybin ÷ SPC:
  - A/B: 24/82 = 29.3%; 82 × 0.297 = 24.4, which rounds to "24".
  - C: 35/118 = 29.7%; 118 × 0.297 = 35.0.
- The DSLD Siliphos label does the same thing (exactly 29.7%).
- So the true silybin content may be higher (up to 36.3%), and the phospholipid content correspondingly lower.

**2. "1:2" is most consistent with a weight ratio.** [C-calc]
- Indena's 29.7–36.3% specification equals 33% ± 10%, which matches 1 part silybin to 2 parts phospholipid by weight (33.3% silybin).
- A 1:2 molar complex would be only about 23.9% silybin (482.44 / (482.44 + 2 × 770)), using MW 770 for PC implied by the patent's "15.4 g (0.020 mole)".
- 1:2 molar is therefore below the commercial specification. The 29.3–36.3% range corresponds to roughly 1.1–1.5 mol of PC per mol of silybin, which is inside the patent's 0.3–2 mol range.

**3. Estimated phospholipid (≈ PC) content** [C-calc]. This assumes SPC contains only silybin plus phospholipid (no carrier):

| | A or B, per tablet | C, per tablet | C, ½ tablet per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upper bound: SPC − label silybin | 58 mg | 83 mg | 41.5 mg |
| Nominal Siliphos 1:2 by weight (66.7% phospholipid) — **best estimate** | **54.7 mg** | **78.7 mg** | **39.3 mg** |
| Lower bound: silybin at specification maximum 36.3% | 52.2 mg | 75.2 mg | 37.6 mg |

- If the phospholipid fraction is a soy lecithin grade that is "mainly" but not 100% PC, true PC would be somewhat lower than these figures (not quantifiable).

**4. PC source.** The classic Siliphos / IdB 1016 phospholipid is soy PC (Kidd & Head; Indena patent). Nothing found says whether the current Indena grade, or the SPC in Denamarin, uses soy or sunflower. Denamarin's labels make no soy declaration. For a soy-allergic dog this cannot be resolved without asking Nutramax.

### Gaps
- Nutramax does not name its SPC supplier, PC content, PC purity, phospholipid source, or any carrier in the SPC.
- Indena's specification sheets (2019 and 2024) also do not give the phospholipid source.
- I could not fetch Siegel's primary reference or an Indena technical data sheet that states whether "1:2" is by weight or by mole.

## Q2. What fraction of oral PC is absorbed (dog vs human vs rat), and how many mg does the dog absorb from A, B and C?

### Takeaway
**Absorption is high in humans and rats.** Oral PC is absorbed at **≥90%** in humans (radiolabel, n = 5) and rats (>90% in 24 h). The mechanism:
1. Pancreatic phospholipase A2 hydrolyzes PC to 1-acyl-lysoPC in the gut lumen.
2. About half is re-esterified to PC in the enterocyte and leaves in lymph chylomicrons, ending up mostly in HDL.
3. The rest is degraded to glycerophosphocholine, choline and fatty acids.

**No canine absorption study was found.** Applying the human/rat ≥90% to the dog [B-human/B-rat, species transfer D]:

| Product | PC absorbed per day | Range | Intact PC re-formed to lymph |
|---|---|---|---|
| A or B | about 49 mg | 47–57 mg | about 25 mg |
| C | about 35 mg | 34–41 mg | about 18 mg |

### Cited Findings
**Human, radiolabel** [B-human] — Zierenberg & Grundy, J Lipid Res 1982;23:1136–1142. [PMID 7175371](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7175371/); DOI 10.1016/s0022-2275(20)38050-0.
- "The metabolic fate of 1 [g] of 3H/14C-labeled dilinoleoglycerophosphocholine was studied in five patients after oral administration. The 3H label was in choline and 14C was in the two linoleic acid residues. More than 90% of both isotopes was absorbed from the intestine."
- "...this suggests that most oral PC was hydrolyzed to lysolecithin before absorption."
- "The mean maximum concentration in total blood volume was 20% of the administered dose for 3H and 28% for 14C."
- "...absorbed PC seemingly was incorporated preferentially into the HDL fraction of plasma."
- The Europe PMC abstract text drops the unit after "1"; a search summary of the same paper gives 1 g.

**Human drug label, Essentiale® forte N** (soy "essential phospholipids") [C-label]. Russian label on Vidal, updated 2025-12-30 — [vidal.ru Essentiale forte N](https://www.vidal.ru/drugs/essentiale_forte_n__1025)
- "Более 90% принятых внутрь фосфолипидов всасывается в тонком кишечнике. Большая часть их расщепляется фосфолипазой А до 1-ацил-лизофосфатидилхолина, 50% которого немедленно подвергается обратному ацетилированию в полиненасыщенный фосфатидилхолин еще в ходе процесса всасывания в слизистой оболочке кишечника. Этот полиненасыщенный фосфатидилхолин с током лимфы попадает в кровь и оттуда, главным образом, в связанном с ЛПВП виде поступает в печень."
  - Translation: more than 90% of oral phospholipids are absorbed in the small intestine. Most are cleaved by phospholipase A to 1-acyl-lysophosphatidylcholine, of which 50% is immediately re-acylated to polyunsaturated PC during absorption in the mucosa. That PC reaches the blood via lymph and goes to the liver mainly bound to HDL.
- "В кале обнаруживается 2% от введенной дозы 3H и 4.5% от введенной дозы 14C, в моче - 6% от 3H … Оба изотопа более чем на 90% всасываются в кишечнике."
  - Translation: 2% of the 3H (choline) and 4.5% of the 14C (fatty acid) label appear in feces; 6% of the 3H appears in urine; both isotopes are more than 90% absorbed.
- The label also gives choline-label half-life 66 h, and fatty-acid-label Cmax up to 27.9% of the dose with half-life 32 h.

**Human review** [B-human] — [Cohn et al., Nutrients 2010, PMC3257636](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3257636/)
- "the biliary pathway delivers 10–20 g of PL and ~1.5 g of cholesterol to the intestinal lumen per day, which is at least two to four times more than that supplied by the diet."
- "Dietary PC is almost completely absorbed (>90%) by the human intestine, and rapidly appears in plasma lipoproteins and red blood cells."
- "PC digestion occurs in the small intestine, since lingual and gastric lipases are incapable of hydrolyzing PC."

**Rat** [B-rat]

After 24 h more than 90% of the applied radioactivity was absorbed from the intestinal tract." The PC was polyunsaturated, given in an oil medium.

### Inferences
**1. Best estimate: about 90% of the PC dose is absorbed** [B-human, n = 5 + B-rat; species transfer D].
- The human fecal recoveries (2% of the choline label, 4.5% of the acyl label) suggest true absorption could be about 95–98%.
- I use 90% as the conservative central value and 90–98% as the range.

**2. Absorbed PC for this dog** [C-calc on Q1 estimates]

| Product | PC dose per day | × 0.90 | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| A or B | 54.7 mg | **≈49 mg** | 47–57 mg (52.2 × 0.90 to 58 × 0.98) |
| C, ½ tablet | 39.3 mg | **≈35 mg** | 34–41 mg |

**3. What form "absorbed" takes.** About half is re-formed into intact (lyso→)PC and carried in lymph chylomicrons/HDL [B-human label, B-rat]. That is about 25 mg/day for A/B and 18 mg/day for C. The remainder enters metabolism as glycerophosphocholine, choline and fatty acids.

**4. Scale against the dog's own bile PC.**
- Human bile delivers 10–20 g of phospholipid per day into the gut [B-human]. The supplement's ~40–55 mg is therefore a very small addition to the intestinal PC pool.
- No canine biliary-PC output figure was found, so this comparison is only qualitative for dogs.

**5. Empty-stomach dosing.** Denamarin is given at least 1 h before a meal; Essentiale is labelled "во время еды" (with meals). No data compare fasting with fed absorption of exogenous PC. Even fasting, the lumen contains bile PC and pancreatic enzymes, so I would not expect a large reduction, but this is unverified [D].

### Gaps
- No canine data on oral PC absorption or lymphatic transport; the dog figures are an extrapolation.
- No data on whether complexation with silybin in the phytosome alters PC digestion or absorption.
- Sources that failed: the Springer chapter (redirected to login), the scilit abstract page (403), the full JLR text (403 to WebFetch), and the EFSA 2017 lecithin re-evaluation on PMC (page body empty).

## Q3. How much choline does this PC supply compared with a 9.1 kg dog's requirement, and does PC at this dose have an established liver-protective role in dogs?

### Takeaway
**Choline:**
- The PC supplies about **7 mg of choline per day from A or B** (range 6.8–7.9) and about **5 mg from C** (range 4.9–5.7).
- The dog's daily choline need at 620 kcal is **211–294 mg**, depending on the guideline (AAFCO 340, FEDIAF 409–474, or NRC 425 mg per 1,000 kcal).
- So the supplement covers only **about 2–3.4%** of the requirement. That is nutritionally negligible.

**PC as a liver-protective agent:**
- This PC dose is about 6 mg/kg/day for A/B and 4.3 mg/kg/day for C.
- That is roughly one-fifth to one-third of the human EPL drug dose per kg (1.8 g EPL/day ≈ 1.44 g PC/day), and less on a metabolic-weight basis.
- No canine study shows a liver-protective effect of PC alone at any dose. The only recent canine trial used a multi-ingredient product with no control group.

### Cited Findings

- "Phosphatidylcholine, which contains about 13% choline by weight, is the main form of choline in dietary products."
- "choline comprises only about 13% of the weight of phosphatidylcholine … Therefore, a supplement containing 4,230 mg (4.23 grams) of phosphatidylcholine would provide 550 mg of choline."
- "Lecithins in processed food have been estimated to increase the daily consumption of phosphatidylcholine by about 1.5 mg/kg of body weight for adults."

**Human EPL dose** [C-label] — [vidal.ru](https://www.vidal.ru/drugs/essentiale_forte_n__1025):

**Only canine phospholipid study found** [A-dog, uncontrolled; multi-component] — Nieradka R, Jank M, Otto S, Kurosad A, Front Vet Sci 2026;13:1938591, [PMC13663177](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13663177/):
- Design: open-label pilot, 17 dogs with asymptomatic raised liver values, 8 weeks of "Hepatiale Forte Advanced", "one tablet per 10 kg body weight, once daily".
- Per tablet: "Soybean seed extract (Glycine max (L.) Merr.), standardized to 98% phospholipids and 20% phosphatidylcholine 70" mg, plus L-arginine 75 mg, L-glutamine 75 mg, betaine 70 mg, and milk thistle extract (60% silybin) 50 mg.
- Results: "statistically significant reductions in ALT (p = 0.004), AST (p = 0.004), ALP (p = 0.001), total bilirubin (p = 0.001) and serum bile acids (p = 0.007)".
- There is no control group, and the effect cannot be attributed to the phospholipid.

**Rat liver-injury models for Siliphos** [B-rat; figure labels only] — [Kidd & Head 2005](https://altmedrev.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/v10-3-193.pdf):

- The text extraction did not give those arms' results.

### Inferences
**1. Choline supplied** [C-calc], at 13% of PC (molecular-weight arithmetic gives 13.3–14.2% for C16–C18 PCs):

| | A or B | C (½ tablet) |
|---|---|---|
| Choline from PC per day | 54.7 × 0.13 = **7.1 mg** (range 6.8–7.9) | 39.3 × 0.13 = **5.1 mg** (range 4.9–5.7) |
| Absorbed at about 90% | ≈6.4 mg | ≈4.6 mg |

**2. The dog's choline requirement at 620 kcal/day** [C-calc]:
- NRC: 425 × 0.62 = **263.5 mg**
- AAFCO adult minimum: 340 × 0.62 = **210.8 mg**
- FEDIAF adult at 110 kcal/kg^0.75: 409 × 0.62 = **253.6 mg**. This column is closest to this dog's ~118 kcal/kg^0.75.
- FEDIAF adult at 95 kcal/kg^0.75: 474 × 0.62 = 293.9 mg

**3. Share of the requirement met by the supplement's choline** [C-calc]:

| | NRC | AAFCO | FEDIAF 110 | FEDIAF 95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A or B (7.1 mg) | 2.7% | 3.4% | 2.8% | 2.4% |
| C (5.1 mg) | 1.9% | 2.4% | 2.0% | 1.7% |

Any complete diet meeting these profiles already supplies roughly 30–58 times more choline than the supplement adds (210.8 ÷ 7.1 = 30; 293.9 ÷ 5.1 = 58). The PC in Denamarin does not matter nutritionally.

**4. PC dose compared with the human EPL drug dose** [C-calc; the 70 kg adult is my assumption]:
- Human: 1.44 g PC/day ÷ 70 kg = 20.6 mg/kg (59.5 mg/kg^0.75).
- Dog, A/B: 6.0 mg/kg (10.4 mg/kg^0.75), which is about **29% per kg** or **18% per kg^0.75** of the human dose.
- Dog, C: 4.3 mg/kg (7.5 mg/kg^0.75), which is about **21% per kg** or **13% per kg^0.75**.
- The PC-only arms in the rat liver models (400–640 mg/kg) were about 67–107 times the dog's ~6 mg/kg/day.
- A/B gives about 4 times the ~1.5 mg/kg/day of PC that human adults get from food lecithin additives (LPI).

**5. Conclusion on PC in Denamarin.** The PC is best understood as the carrier that makes silybin absorbable (the phytosome's purpose; see Filburn 2007). It should not be treated as a third active ingredient at a meaningful liver-protective dose for dogs. No veterinary evidence supports a liver-protective effect of PC alone at roughly 4–6 mg/kg/day [D: absence of evidence].

### Gaps
- No controlled canine study of PC or EPL alone was found. The Nieradka 2026 pilot is uncontrolled and multi-ingredient.
- The results of the PC-only rat arms in Kidd & Head's Figure 6 were not extracted.
- The guideline choline values are minimum recommended dietary levels, not physiological requirements. The dog's actual diet choline content is unknown.

## Q5. Terminology (English / Chinese) with authoritative sources and specification ranges

### Takeaway
- **Milk thistle** = 水飞蓟, the plant *Silybum marianum* (L.) Gaertn. (菊科, Compositae/Asteraceae). The Chinese Pharmacopoeia (ChP) drug material "水飞蓟" is its dried ripe fruit (水飞蓟果实; Latin Silybi Fructus).
- **Silymarin** = 水飞蓟素, the flavonolignan mixture extracted from the fruit.
- **Silybin / silibinin** = 水飞蓟宾 (C25H22O10). It is a 1:1 mixture of silybin A and silybin B (水飞蓟宾A/B) and is the main constituent, about 40–70% of silymarin.
- **Specifications:**
  - ChP 2020 fruit: ≥0.60% 水飞蓟宾 on a dried basis.
  - USP powdered fruit: ≥2.0% silymarin calculated as silybin.
  - USP extract: silybin A+B is 40–65% of the silymarin.
  - Fruit contains up to about 6% silymarin; commercial extracts are mostly standardized to 70–80% silymarin.
- **Phosphatidylcholine** = 磷脂酰胆碱. **Lecithin** = 卵磷脂, a mixture of phospholipids; in biochemistry it is a synonym of PC.
- **SAMe** = S-腺苷甲硫氨酸 / S-腺苷蛋氨酸 / 腺苷蛋氨酸 (ademetionine). The Chinese prescription drug salt is 丁二磺酸腺苷蛋氨酸 (1,4-butanedisulfonate; 思美泰 Transmetil). The Chinese-name sources for SAMe are weaker; see the grades below.

- milk-thistle extract (80% silymarin) ≈ 0.32–0.56 × its mass as silybin
- SPC ≈ 0.297 × its mass as silybin

#### Reading the table
- **A and B are identical** on every active-type ingredient, including PC. They differ only in dosage form, flavor/sweetener, and the named SAMe salt.
